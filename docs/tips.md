## Easy Access to the Side Panel

For quick access to Tab Stash, use the toolbar button or right-click the toolbar
button and choose "_Show Stashed Tabs in Side Panel_".

You can also load the list of stashed tabs by right-clicking anywhere on the
page, selecting "_Tab Stash_" from the context menu, and choosing "_Show
Stashed Tabs in a Tab_".

## Make Tab Stash Your Homepage

If you prefer the full-browser view of your stashed tabs, you can make Tab
Stash your homepage.

1. Right-click the Tab Stash icon in the toolbar, and choose "Show Stashed Tabs
   in a Tab".
2. Copy the URL from the address bar.
3. Open your browser settings.
4. Open the startup or homepage settings.
5. Choose a custom URL.
6. Paste the copied Tab Stash URL.

You can now open the stash any time you like by clicking the "Home" button in
your browser toolbar.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

You can customize these shortcuts--[here's how][wiki-shortcuts].

[wiki-shortcuts]: https://github.com/josh-berry/tab-stash/wiki/Changing-Keyboard-Shortcuts

On **Mac**:

- Show stashed tabs in side panel: _Ctrl+Shift+S_
- Toolbar action: _Ctrl+Shift+T_

On **Windows**, **Linux** and other platforms:

- Show stashed tabs in side panel: _Alt+Shift+S_
- Toolbar action: _Alt+Shift+T_

## Stashing Only Selected Tabs

If you get distracted and wind up with a bunch of tabs mixed together in your
window for different tasks, you can select only those tabs applicable to a
particular task and stash them, leaving the remaining tabs open.

Just Shift-click (or Ctrl/Cmd-click) in the tab bar to select multiple tabs at
once, and click any "Stash all..." button. When Tab Stash sees that you have
multiple tabs selected, it will stash only the selected tabs.

You can still stash individual tabs using the "Stash this tab" buttons in the
stash view; these buttons ignore multi-selection and stash only the
currently-visible tab.

## Exporting Tabs from Tab Stash

Tab Stash stores all saved tabs as bookmarks. Restoring a tab through the _Tab
Stash_ interface will first search for a matching open tab. If there is no
matching tab, Tab Stash will open a new tab.

There are two ways to get your saved tabs out of Tab Stash:

1. Tab Stash comes with import/export for a variety of formats. In the Tab Stash
   UI, click the menu icon to the left of the search box and choose "Export...".
2. Use your browser or another extension to directly access your bookmarks. You
   can do this even if Tab Stash is not working or has been uninstalled. Tab
   Stash places bookmarks for all saved tabs under _Other Bookmarks > Tab
   Stash_.

You can find detailed instructions for exporting your stashed tabs
[on the wiki][export].

[export]: https://github.com/josh-berry/tab-stash/wiki

## Manually Editing Tab Stash Bookmarks

You may freely delete, move, or edit bookmarks saved by Tab Stash using the
built-in bookmark editor, or any other bookmarks extension you like. Tab Stash
will notice manual changes to your bookmarks.
